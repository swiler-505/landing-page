The case for it

The argument should be about finance, not transcription.

Cost of capital is a particularly expensive and litigious component of utility rate cases. Rather than debate and answer questions at the crux of the argument, such as what is a reasonable terminal growth rate, what is the right beta, or whether additional risk factors should be considered, much work from cost of capital witnesses involves rather menial data entry and logging.

TURBOROE moves that repetitive collection work into a reproducible Python workflow, leaving more room for the questions that deserve professional judgment. The aim is not to automate conviction. It is to make the analytical trail easier to reproduce, interrogate, and improve.

A stated position

The package is for the quality of the analysis, not for a side of the table.

TURBOROE is not intended to serve only company witnesses, staff, or consumer advocates. It follows the methods where they lead. At present, the application of multi-stage DCF, a modest market risk premium, and a rejection of Morin's risk premium methods produces a cost of equity below the current status quo.

The invitation

Try the package. Argue with the assumptions.

Consider using this open-source package for a free and efficient return on equity analysis for regulatory ratemaking purposes. Despite the inherent uncertainty in estimating a utility return on equity, the standards set forth in Hope and Bluefield delineate a more straightforward path than many consultants would have you believe.
